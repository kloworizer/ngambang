An island that floats on the edge of your screen.

Ngambang is a small orb for Windows. It rests on any screen edge, shows what is running on its ring, and unfolds into soft slips for music, volume, your calendar, markets, weather, prayer times, tasks, builds, ports, your clipboard and games. When a game or a video goes fullscreen it moves to your other display, or sinks to a 3 px line. Prefer a notch? It can hang from the top of the screen instead.

How it behaves

Four states, from a line to a card.

SEAMOut of the way

A 3 px line while a fullscreen app has focus. With a second display it moves there instead and comes back when you leave fullscreen. Mic/camera alerts, your break, meetings and prayer times still come through.

ORBResting

The ring is the tide: progress of the track, timer or build that is running. Scroll on it to turn the player up or down; the level shows as you go.

TAGA glance

Hover, or a new event, and a label pops out: the next track with its cover and controls, a meeting in ten minutes, a change in the weather, a finished build.

SLIPSEverything

Click and it slides out like a drawer: a card sized to the module, with the most important thing largest, in one column or two side by side for tall ones docked top or bottom. Each module gets a tab in the rail along the bottom; scroll to flip and it slides to the next.

New in 0.1.7 & 0.1.8

An orb or a notch. One display or three.

Notch at the top

Pick Notch at the top in Settings or the tray's Dock edge menu. The island hangs from the top of the screen like a notch and slides left or right when you drag it. It shows the song that is playing, or turns through the weather, your watchlist, your next task, event and prayer time.

dock.style = "notch"

Out of the way of fullscreen

With two or more displays, a fullscreen video or game sends the island to another display, where it stays a full orb with its tags. Leave fullscreen and it goes back where it was. On by default.

dock.move_in_fullscreen = true

Reminders that arrive

Calendar reminders use the ones you set on each event, prayer reminders count down to the minute, and both now show over fullscreen apps and catch up after sleep or a clock change.

VALARM · clock re-checked every minute

Compact strips

Docked to the top or bottom, every slip is a low, wide strip at most 180 px tall, tabs included, with the headline large and the details small. Docked left or right, slips are narrower too.

Modules

Keep what you use. Switch off the rest.

There are no bundles or profiles. Each module turns on or off on its own, from the Settings window, the tray menu or the config file, and a module that is off starts nothing. Try it below.

Your [modules] block

Paste it into %APPDATA%\Ngambang\config.toml. The island picks up the change as soon as you save, no restart.


        

      

    

  


  

    

      
Your day

      
The next meeting, the market, the sky.

      
A glance at the edge of the screen instead of five browser tabs. Everything loads in the background, works offline with the last data, and costs nothing when the module is off.

      

        

          
Calendar

          
Indonesian public holidays are there from the start. Link Google Calendar, Outlook or iCloud by their private iCal address, as many calendars as you like, each in its own colour. See this week, what is next, and get the reminders you set on each event, with a Join button for meetings.

          
iCal · recurring events and time zones

        

        

          
Markets

          
Type a name in Settings, from BBCA to bitcoin to USD/IDR, and pick it. The slip shows price, today's change, a sparkline and the latest headlines from Google News and Yahoo, refreshed every few minutes. A tag pops up when something on your watchlist moves 3 % (or your own step) and when fresh news about it breaks.

          
Yahoo Finance · Google News · may be delayed

        

        

          
Weather & warnings

          
The weather for your place and the next hours, plus earthquake, tsunami, cyclone and flood warnings nearby. A change in the sky or a warning pops up.

          
Open-Meteo · GDACS · USGS

        

        

          
Jadwal sholat & to-do

          
Prayer times calculated on your computer for about 240 places, with a reminder before each one that shows even over a fullscreen app. And a short to-do list you type straight into the slip.

          
Kemenag RI, MWL, ISNA, Umm al-Qura and more

        

      

    

  


  

    

      
Media

      
Control every app that makes sound.

      
Windows has two separate hooks for this, and Ngambang uses both, so nothing playing is out of reach.

      

        

          
Playback

          
Title, artist, album art, play, pause, skip and seek for every app that publishes Windows media controls: music apps, every major browser (so web players too) and video players. The now-playing tag carries the controls too; with several playing, switch between them from source chips.

          
Windows.Media.Control · SMTC

        

        

          
Volume & mute

          
A mixer row with its level for every process that outputs audio, including games and voice chat that report nothing else. Scroll on the orb and the player you are listening to gets louder or quieter, not the whole system.

          
WASAPI · IAudioSessionManager2

        

      

    

  


  

    

      
For your terminal

      
Know when the build is done.

      

        

          
Wrap a command with ngambang run, or add one line to your shell profile and every command that takes longer than 30 seconds reports how it ended. A failed one gets a button that brings its terminal back.

          

            cargo test 2m14s
            npm run build exit 1
          

        

        

          

            
            
          

          


# once, in $PROFILE
ngambang hook powershell | Out-String | Invoke-Expression

# any time
ngambang run -- cargo build --release
ngambang notify "deploy staging" --progress 40
ngambang done "deploy staging" --ok

          

          


# once, in ~/.bashrc or ~/.zshrc (Git Bash, WSL)
eval "$(ngambang.exe hook bash)"   # or: hook zsh

# any time
ngambang.exe run -- make test
ngambang.exe notify "backup finished" --level ok
